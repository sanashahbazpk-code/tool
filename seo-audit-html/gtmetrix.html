1. Test

Gain a clear, complete view of your site’s real performance

Go beyond a single score. See exactly what’s slow, why it’s slow, and what to fix first.

  • Full Lighthouse-powered reports with Web Vitals (LCP, TBT, CLS) and structure audits
  • Real User Metrics from CrUX data — see how actual visitors experience your site, not just a lab test
  • Waterfall, video, and filmstrip views to pinpoint the exact request or script causing the slowdown
  • Test the way your visitors actually experience your site

    • 55+ simulated mobile and tablet devices (iPhone, Galaxy, Pixel, and more)

2. Monitor

Catch performance problems before your users do

Schedule recurring tests and get alerted the moment something regresses.

  • Hourly, daily, weekly, or monthly automated testing with historical trend graphs
  • Instant alerts by email when a page slows down or fails to load
  • Track Web Vitals, GTmetrix Grade, and/or page size and requests over time to catch performance bloat

4. Integrate with AI

Bring performance data straight into your AI workflow

Fast to Build Isn’t the Same as Fast to Load

  • AI tools are good at shipping features quickly – but they’re not necessarily optimized for performance.
  • Continuous iteration often brings more pages, widgets, and polish, increasing bloat and worsening performance.
  • Connecting GTmetrix MCP to your AI tool brings real-world assessment from real page loads, in real locations around the globe.

Ask in plain language. Get real data, right in your chat.

  • Connect GTmetrix MCP to any AI coding tool – Claude, Cursor, GitHub Copilot CLI, ChatGPT, Lovable, and more – no dashboard required
  • Ask questions like “Why is my LCP slow?” and get a real, specific answer pulled from actual GTmetrix test data
  • Apply a fix, then rerun the test in the same conversation to confirm it worked, without switching tools or tabs
