Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our product and billing.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade or switch plans at any time as your needs evolve. Whether you want to add more domains, enable advanced tracking features, or access higher-tier reporting limits, plan changes are flexible and reflected in your billing upon update.

Can I white-label reports for my clients?

Yes — SEO Site Checkup's Website and Professional Plans supports white-label reports specifically for agencies and teams. You can generate branded SEO reports with your own logo and styling so you can present insights professionally to clients.

Do I need technical SEO knowledge to use this tool?

No — the platform is designed for both beginners and seasoned SEO professionals. It automatically scans and prioritizes issues for you, explains each finding in plain English, and provides actionable guidance so you don’t have to be a technical expert to benefit from the results.

How is SEO Site Checkup different from other tools?

SEO Site Checkup stands out because it combines traditional SEO auditing (technical issues, meta tags, site structure) with AI visibility tracking across major LLM platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and others. It’s geared toward modern search — not just Google rankings but also how your brand shows up inside AI-powered search and chat experiences.

You can check how SEO Site Checkup compares to other tools here.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes, you can try us for free for 7 days. After the trial ends, you will be billed and you can cancel your plan at any time. If you want, we’ll provide you with a free, personalized 30-minute onboarding call to get you up and running as soon as possible.

What if I want to manage more domains?

You can add and manage multiple domains from a single dashboard. Higher tier plans let you track more sites and provide centralized reporting and monitoring across all of them — ideal for agencies, networks of sites, or businesses with multiple brands.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Upon cancellation, you’ll retain access through the end of your current billing period, and you won’t be charged again going forward. For full details on refunds or prorated credits, check the tool’s Refund Policy / Terms of Service.

What’s included in the AI visibility tracking?

AI visibility tracking shows how your brand or domain appears inside AI engines and LLM responses across platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and more. It includes:

  • Brand mention frequency in AI search responses
  • Citation context (which prompts mention you)
  • Competitive comparison of AI visibility
  • Insights into how AI models interpret your content versus competitors


This goes beyond traditional keyword rankings to help you understand and improve visibility in AI-driven discovery and recommendations.
